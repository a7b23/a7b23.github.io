Working on post-training at Google DeepMind to improve model behavior that affects frontier capabilities across Gemini.

I am currently a Staff Software Engineer at Google DeepMind, working on post-training to improve model behavior that affects capabilities. I was a core contributor to the Gemini Argon, Carbon, Hydrogen, and Neon launches. Previously, I worked on Image Generation in Gemini — serving as post-training co-lead and pre-training core contributor for Nano Banana Pro (keynote), and core contributor to Nano Banana, Imagen 4, and the first native image generation launch in Gemini. Earlier, I also contributed to Gemini 1.5 and Gemini 2.5 through Multimodal Post-training.

Prior to DeepMind, I worked on the Perception Team at Waymo improving data efficiency and fine-tuning LVLMs for autonomous driving. I hold an MS in Computer Science from Stanford University (2021), where I was a Research Assistant with Prof. Stefano Ermon — developing one of the earliest latent diffusion models and winning the ICLR 2022 Best Paper Award. Before Stanford, I built deep visual search systems at Adobe India (CVPR Workshop 2019 Best Paper) after graduating from IIT Kharagpur.
